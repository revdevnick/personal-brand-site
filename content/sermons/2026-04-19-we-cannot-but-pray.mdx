Last week they could not but speak. This week they cannot but pray. Acts 4:23–31 follows the church from the council into a room that answers pressure with Scripture, boldness, and the Spirit again — and leaves three questions close to home: Where do you run when pressure hits? Do you pray His Word back to Him? Will you ask for more of the hard thing He has already called you to?
