Peter writes as servant and apostle to those who share the same precious faith. Divine power has already supplied what life and godliness require; the question is whether we will keep adding to that faith — or grow blind, forgetful of forgiveness, and stuck. The passage leaves heart-checks close to home: Do you know who you are? Are you relying on what God has already provided? Is the chain of virtues growing? Is your calling being confirmed — moving forward, or have you hit the wall?
