The apostles return spent, John's death still heavy, and the crowd will not wait. Jesus invites them to withdraw and rest before they engage again — then meets interruption with compassion. Mark 6:30–46 leaves a searching question close to home: which ring of your life is empty, and do you know the times of refreshing that follow when that rhythm is restored?
