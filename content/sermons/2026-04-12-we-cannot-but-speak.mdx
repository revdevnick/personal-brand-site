Rulers are astonished. The healed man stands where denial should be. Threats come, and Peter and John answer with respectful holy defiance: we cannot but speak. Acts 4:13–22 presses three heart-checks close to home: Have you been with Jesus? Can you keep from speaking what you have seen and heard? Where does silence win under pressure?
