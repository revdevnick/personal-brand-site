Acts 9:20–43 moves from a persecutor who immediately proclaims Christ, to a fisherman who speaks healing in Jesus' name, to a seamstress whose life and raising draw many to faith. Between them the church finds peace and multiplies. The passage leaves three heart-checks close to home: Are you waiting on credentials before you obey? What is already in your hands? Who have you written off as too far gone?
