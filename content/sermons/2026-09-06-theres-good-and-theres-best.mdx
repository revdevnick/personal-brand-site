Asia looked right. Bithynia looked right. The Spirit said no to both — not because the work was unworthy, but because good is not always best. Then Macedonia broke open in a vision, and Paul answered without delay. Acts 16:6–10 asks a searching question: will you cling to every good door, or stay attentive enough to walk through the one God opens?
