From Iconium to Lystra to Derbe and home again, the pattern holds: sow, suffer, strengthen, appoint, report. God opened a door of faith — and left us with the harder question. Are we willing to leave these doors and go? Loyal to the 1979 rebuild, or to what He is building now? From y'all come to we go.
