Acts 8:26–40 follows Philip from the city to a chariot on a wilderness road. One question opens Isaiah 53 to Jesus. Water appears — and a man asks what prevents him from being baptized. The passage leaves three heart-checks close to home: Will you go when the Spirit says go? Will you open a door with one question? What is stopping you from belief, baptism, or obedience?
