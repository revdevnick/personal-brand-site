Kittyhawk (later Aloft) built tools for drone operations. I helped ship Air Control, the early Kittyhawk app, and B4UFLY — the airspace app many United States drone manuals still point to.

The problem was safety and clarity in the air. The work was maps, regulations, and a team. I do not tell it as a trophy. It was a job I was given, and we tried to do it well.
