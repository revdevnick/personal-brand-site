title: Food Genie
problem: People who could not decide where to eat needed an unbiased third party, not another list.
craft: Swift, location, and a wheel — later an App Store listing.
order: 4

Food Genie started as a small web tool for coworkers who could not pick lunch. It became an iOS app: six local places, a spin, a landing.

It was a student project that shipped. PhoneDog listed it. It is not the most important thing I have made. It is a clean example of a small human problem and a small piece of software.
