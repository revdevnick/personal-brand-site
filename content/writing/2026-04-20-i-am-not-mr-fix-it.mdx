---
title: "I am not Mr. Fix-It"
date: 2026-04-20
tags: [faith, tech]
excerpt: I love solving problems. That is not the same as being the one who saves.
---

I like a problem I can hold. A failing test. A scoreboard that will not talk to the phone. A form that loses a family's name. Give me enough time and coffee and I will usually find a way through.

There are other problems. Grief. Calling. The 2 a.m. question that does not have a stack trace. I have wanted, more than once, to be useful there the same way I am useful in a codebase. I am not. I am a pastor and a builder, and I am a man. The Lord is the one who heals.

This site exists so you can find the work — sermons, writing, the technical craft — and so you can see past me. If you came looking for Nick Perkins, you are welcome. If you came looking for Jesus, you are in the right place. The first is a doorway. The second is the house.
